# Code of Conduct

We want participation in OSS Maintainer Check to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or viewpoint.

Examples of expected behavior include using considerate language, focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than people, accepting good-faith feedback, and helping keep discussions relevant and accessible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Reporting

Do not respond publicly to harassment. Contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub's contact options on the maintainer's profile and include a link or a concise description. The maintainer will review reports as promptly and fairly as possible and may remove content or restrict participation. This policy applies to project spaces and public interactions when someone is representing the project.

This code of conduct is a project-specific statement of expectations, not a claim of affiliation with another organization or code-of-conduct provider.
